Our Project Mission

WakeQuake was born from a common challenge faced by students and busy professionals: waking up on time reliably. By combining physical haptic vibrations, cognitive interactive challenges (math, geography, and riddles), and a private, offline computer vision sensor, WakeQuake ensures you are out of bed and ready for the day ahead.
